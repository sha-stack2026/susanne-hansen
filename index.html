Letter

Dear Waabi Hiring Team,

I am writing to apply for the Executive Assistant position in Waabi’s Office of the CEO. Your posting describes work I love, at a company whose mission I genuinely care about.

I bring the balance this role requires: deep attention to detail at a high volume and pace, sound judgment when the information is incomplete, and genuine ease with change. I anticipate needs before they become issues, see commitments through, and am willing to push back when the executive’s time or the quality of the work calls for it.

When a restructuring at the Village Church moved strategic partnership out of my role, I chose to step away and spend this year learning to put AI to work. Because the tools change almost daily, I approached it as I would any operating problem: understand how the pieces fit, then run real work through them. Running my own team of AI agents put me on the other side of executive support. I decide what gets handed off, what comes back to me for a decision, and what a useful briefing looks like when my own time is the constraint. That has sharpened how I support an executive. I am still learning, and I am eager to keep looking for the places where AI can genuinely help a team.

I measure my work by how well it serves the people I support, not by whether anyone sees it, and discretion is second nature to me. On a team, I am lighthearted and easy to work with, focused on the details, and always looking for the next small improvement in how the work gets done.

Autonomous transportation is personal for me, and I am its self-appointed ambassador to anyone who will listen. I am an optimist about where it is headed, and putting my work behind a team that is bringing autonomous trucks and robotaxis to the road would mean a great deal to me.

I would welcome the chance to talk about the experience, operational capacity, and genuine, flexible support I can bring to your team. Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,

Susanne Hansen
